## What time can I check in?

From 15:00. If you arrive earlier we will hold your bags at reception and call
you as soon as the room is ready. Early check-in before 13:00 is possible when
we have the room free, but we cannot promise it in advance.

## Do you have parking?

Not on site. The public car park at Example Square is a two minute walk and
costs about EUR 15 for 24 hours. There is no need to book it.

## Is breakfast included?

It depends on the rate you booked. If it is not included you can add it at
reception or on the morning, EUR 18 per person. Children under 12 eat free.
Served 07:30 to 10:30.

## Can I cancel my booking?

Free up to 48 hours before your arrival date. Inside 48 hours we charge the
first night. If you booked through an agency, cancel with them rather than us.

## Do you allow pets?

Small dogs are welcome, EUR 15 a night. Please tell us before you arrive so we
can give you a suitable room. Assistance dogs stay free and need no notice.

## Is there air conditioning?

Yes, in every room, individually controlled.

## Can I have a quiet room?

Rooms on the courtyard side are quieter. We will note the request on your
booking and do our best, but we cannot guarantee a specific room.

## How far is the airport?

About 20 minutes by taxi, usually EUR 20 to EUR 25. There is also a metro; take
it to Example station and walk five minutes.

## Do you have family rooms?

Yes, they sleep four: one double bed and two singles. Cots are free on request
and an extra bed is EUR 35 a night.

## Can I get a late check-out?

Check-out is 11:00. We can usually offer 14:00 for EUR 30 if the room is not
needed. Ask at reception on the morning of departure.

## Is the hotel accessible?

Yes. There is a lift to every floor and two ground floor rooms have roll-in
showers. Tell us what you need when you book and we will make sure it works.
